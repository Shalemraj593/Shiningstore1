FAQ

Shipping FAQs
How much does shipping cost?
We offer Free Standard Shipping on all orders over $50. For orders under $50, standard shipping is $4.99. Express Shipping is available for a flat rate of $9.99.
How long will it take to receive my order?
Standard shipping typically takes 5–8 business days depending on your location. Express shipping takes 2–3 business days. Please allow 1-2 business days for order processing before shipment.
How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking link. You can also track your package directly from your Account Dashboard under "Order History" or by visiting our Tracking Page.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship globally! International shipping rates and delivery times are calculated at checkout based on your destination country.
Cancellation FAQs
Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placement, provided they have not yet been processed or shipped. You can cancel directly from your Account -> Order History.
What if my order has already shipped?
If your order has already shipped, it cannot be cancelled. However, you can initiate a return within 30 days of receiving the item using our free returns portal.
Will I get a full refund if I cancel?
Yes, if an order is successfully cancelled before shipment, you will receive a full refund to your original payment method within 3-5 business days.
Payment FAQs
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. All transactions are securely encrypted.
Can I pay in my local currency?
Yes, our website supports currency switching. You can select your preferred currency (USD or INR) from the top right corner of the website or the menu on mobile.
When will I be charged for my order?
Your payment method will be authorized and charged immediately upon placing the order to secure your items.
Returns FAQs
What is your return policy?
We offer easy 30-day returns on all items. To be eligible for a return, your item must be unworn, unwashed, and in the exact condition you received it, with all original tags attached.
How do I start a return?
Log into your account, go to "Order History," select the order containing the item you wish to return, and click "Start Return." You will be provided with a prepaid shipping label.
Is return shipping free?
Yes! We provide free return shipping on all domestic orders. For international returns, shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer.
How long does a refund take?
Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days to your original payment method.
Account & Shopping
Do I need an account to place an order?
No, you can check out as a guest. However, creating an account allows you to save addresses, track orders easily, and build a Wishlist.
How do I use the Wishlist?
Simply click the heart icon (♥) on any product to save it to your Wishlist. You can access your saved items at any time by clicking the heart icon in the navigation bar.
How do I reset my password?
On the Sign In modal, click "Forgot Password?". Enter your email address, and we will send you a link to reset your password securely.
